# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone — regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- **Be kind.** Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- **Assume good faith.** Most "weird" questions come from people who are new and confused, not from trolls.
- **Give context.** "This is broken" is hard to act on. "On Windows 11, when I click X, I get Y" is actionable.
- **Help others learn.** If someone is asking a beginner question, treat it as an opportunity, not a nuisance.
- **Use whatever language you want** in issues, but English is preferred for the long-term record (so others can search for it later).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats — in any language
- Doxxing or publishing others' private info
- Trolling, sustained off-topic, deliberate disruption
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery
- Any conduct that would get you fired from a reasonable workplace

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will:
1. **Warn** the offender in private, citing this document.
2. **Edit / hide** the offending content.
3. **Temporarily ban** if the behavior repeats.
4. **Permanently ban** in extreme cases (threats, doxxing, etc.).

Ban = blocked from issues, PRs and discussions. We try to act proportionally; we err on the side of *less* drama, not more.

## Reporting

If you're being harassed, or you see someone else being harassed, contact the maintainers:
- Open a private security advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) — only the maintainers will see it.
- Or DM a maintainer on whatever platform they list in their GitHub profile.

We take all reports seriously. We won't publish names of reporters without their consent.
